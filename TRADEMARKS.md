# Trademark Notice

The icons in `icons/brands/` are **stylized approximations** of third-party trademarks, hand-drawn at 24×24 from memory and intuition. They are not official assets and are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of the companies whose marks they evoke.

## What this means

All product names, logos, and brands referenced by file name in `icons/brands/` are property of their respective owners. Use of these names and visual references is for identification purposes only and does not imply endorsement.

The icons themselves are original artwork, but they are intentionally designed to be **recognizable as** specific brands. This places them in a legal gray area that other brand-icon libraries (e.g., Simple Icons) also occupy.

## Suggested use

Suitable:
- Personal projects, side projects, prototypes
- Internal tools and documentation
- Design exploration and mockups
- Tutorials, articles, and educational material

Less suitable without further consideration:
- Commercial products distributed at scale
- Anywhere users could reasonably mistake the icon for an official asset
- Marketing materials suggesting affiliation
- Resale or relicensing of the brand icons themselves

For production work, prefer **official brand assets** from each company's brand or press page. Most major companies publish brand guidelines and downloadable logo kits.

## Removal requests

If you are a rights holder and want a specific brand icon removed from this pack, open an issue or PR. Removals will be honored without argument.
